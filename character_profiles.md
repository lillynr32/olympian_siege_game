# ⚡ Olympian Siege: Character Profiles & Visual Guide

## Olympian Gods & Goddesses

### 1. ⚡ ZEUS - The Thunder King
**Role:** King of the Gods  
**Health:** 100/100  
**Attack Power:** 25  
**Special Ability:** Lightning Strike  

**Visual Description:**
- **Crown:** Golden crown with lightning bolt emblem
- **Weapon:** Master Bolt (glowing golden staff)
- **Armor:** White and gold ceremonial armor
- **Aura:** Blue electrical crackles around the body
- **Cape:** Purple robe with gold trim and clouds
- **Symbol:** Lightning bolt ⚡

**Personality:** Authoritative, commanding, powerful. The leader of the Olympians.

---

### 2. 🛡️ ATHENA - The Warrior Goddess
**Role:** Goddess of Wisdom & War  
**Health:** 85/85  
**Attack Power:** 20  
**Special Ability:** Strategic Defense  

**Visual Description:**
- **Helmet:** Golden helmet with owl crest
- **Eyes:** Piercing grey/silver eyes
- **Armor:** Silver and bronze battle armor
- **Weapon:** Spear (gleaming with wisdom runes)
- **Shield:** Aegis shield with Medusa head emblem
- **Symbol:** Owl 🦉

**Personality:** Intelligent, calculating, tactical. The brains of the operation.

---

### 3. ⚔️ ARES - The War God
**Role:** God of War & Bloodlust  
**Health:** 90/90  
**Attack Power:** 28 (HIGHEST)  
**Special Ability:** War Fury  

**Visual Description:**
- **Helmet:** Black iron helmet with red plume
- **Body:** Massive muscular frame, battle-scarred
- **Armor:** Dark red and black warrior armor
- **Weapon:** Massive battle axe with blood-red edge
- **Scars:** Battle marks across face and arms
- **Symbol:** Sword crossed with axe ⚔️

**Personality:** Aggressive, fierce, battle-hungry. The strongest attacker.

---

### 4. 🌊 POSEIDON - The Ocean Lord
**Role:** God of the Seas  
**Health:** 95/95  
**Attack Power:** 22  
**Special Ability:** Tidal Wave  

**Visual Description:**
- **Crown:** Trident crown with sea-green jewels
- **Weapon:** Trident (glowing with ocean energy)
- **Armor:** Sea-blue and silver scale armor
- **Cape:** Flowing water-like cloak that moves constantly
- **Aura:** Water droplets and mist surrounding
- **Symbol:** Trident 🔱

**Personality:** Commanding, temperamental, fluid. Master of adaptation.

---

### 5. 💖 APHRODITE - The Love Goddess
**Role:** Goddess of Love & Beauty  
**Health:** 70/70 (LOWEST)  
**Attack Power:** 15 (LOWEST)  
**Special Ability:** Charm Spell  

**Visual Description:**
- **Hair:** Long flowing golden hair
- **Dress:** Silken rose-pink gown
- **Accessories:** Jeweled tiara with hearts
- **Weapon:** Golden bow with rose arrows
- **Aura:** Pink sparkles and rose petals swirl around
- **Symbol:** Heart 💕

**Personality:** Graceful, charming, mysterious. The strategic support.

---

### 6. ☀️ APOLLO - The Sun God
**Role:** God of Sun & Prophecy  
**Health:** 80/80  
**Attack Power:** 18  
**Special Ability:** Solar Beam  

**Visual Description:**
- **Hair:** Golden flowing hair
- **Armor:** Golden and white celestial armor
- **Weapon:** Bow of light (shoots golden arrows)
- **Aura:** Constant golden glow, rays of sun
- **Cloak:** White silk with golden sun patterns
- **Symbol:** Sun ☀️

**Personality:** Artistic, enlightened, graceful. The balanced warrior.

## 🏰 Fortresses

### 1. TROY - The Legendary Fortress
**Defense:** 150/150  
**Resources:** 500  
**Garrison:** 100 troops  

**Visual:**
- Towering wooden palisades with bronze gates
- The famous Trojan Horse at the entrance (as monument)
- High watchtowers with circling eagles
- Multiple concentric walls
- **Color Scheme:** Brown and bronze

---

### 2. SPARTA - The Warrior Haven
**Defense:** 120/120  
**Resources:** 800 (MOST)  
**Garrison:** 150 troops (LARGEST)  

**Visual:**
- Minimalist stone fortress with red banners
- Massive training grounds visible inside
- Hoplite statues at gates
- Red stone architecture
- **Color Scheme:** Red and grey stone

---

### 3. ATHENS - The Intellectual Citadel
**Defense:** 100/100 (LOWEST)  
**Resources:** 600  
**Garrison:** 80 troops (SMALLEST)  

**Visual:**
- Acropolis-style structure with marble columns
- Parthenon replica at center
- Scholarly scrolls depicted on walls
- Golden roof accents
- **Color Scheme:** White marble and gold

---

### 4. OLYMPUS - The Divine Throne (BOSS)
**Defense:** 200/200 (HIGHEST)  
**Resources:** 1000 (MOST)  
**Garrison:** 200 troops (LARGEST)  

**Visual:**
- Massive cloud-shrouded citadel in the sky
- Multiple divine spires reaching toward heavens
- Golden architecture with heavenly glow
- Throne room visible at center
- Rainbow bridge approach
- **Color Scheme:** Gold, white, and rainbow glow

## 🎨 Visual Design Elements

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** #667eea (Divine Purple)
- **Secondary:** #764ba2 (Dark Olympus)
- **Accent:** #4CAF50 (Health Green), #2196F3 (Defense Blue)
- **Warning:** #F44336 (Defeat Red)

### Typography
- **Headers:** Bold, Serif (like Greek inscriptions)
- **Body:** Clean, readable sans-serif
- **Special Text:** Italicized for abilities

### Animations
- Lightning bolt flashes for Zeus attacks
- Water ripples for Poseidon attacks
- Red explosion effect for Ares
- Heart particles for Aphrodite
- Golden glow for Apollo
- Strategic blue aura for Athena
